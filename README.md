# El Precio del Silencio
### Antes de votar · Recuerda

A scrollytelling web experience about Colombia's 2016 peace plebiscite 
and the importance of voting in the 2026 presidential election.

---

## About

El Precio del Silencio is an interactive narrative website built for 
BridgED — a digital platform that uses storytelling to connect Colombian 
youth with their country's history.

The site guides the user through the story of Colombia's peace process, 
the 2016 plebiscite result, and the human cost of not voting — ending 
with a call to action for the 2026 presidential election.

---

## How it works

The experience is entirely scroll-driven. Every animation, transition 
and piece of content is triggered by the user's scroll position — 
nothing plays automatically.

The user moves through:
1. A spinning globe that rotates and zooms into Colombia
2. A newspaper moment from October 3, 2016
3. A vote counter showing the 53,894 vote margin
4. The yes/no plebiscite results
5. Three testimonies from people who couldn't or didn't vote
6. A timeline from 2016 to 2026
7. A call to action to verify their voting location

---

## Built with

- **D3.js** — renders the interactive globe using geographic projections
- **GSAP + ScrollTrigger** — drives all scroll-based animations
- **TopoJSON** — converts world map data into drawable shapes
- **HTML / CSS / Vanilla JavaScript** — no frameworks
